About

I am an interdisciplinary researcher with academic and applied research experience spanning African languages and linguistics, African studies, Black entrepreneurship, economic participation, and community‑based research.

I hold an MA in Linguistics and a First Class Honours BA in Linguistics and Arabic, with peer‑reviewed publications on the syntax of Gã, a Ghanaian language, as well as research on Black entrepreneurship in Canada.

I have over four years of research experience in a university business‑school environment, complemented by experience in African Studies, stakeholder engagement, field research, qualitative data collection, research reporting, and project coordination.

Currently, I am developing a doctoral research agenda at the intersection of African development, governance, public policy, economic participation, institutions, and African narratives and storytelling.
